Gutters overflowing every time it rains? Water pooling by the foundation, or a section pulling away from the fascia? In Michigan, gutters do more than catch rain — they carry snowmelt and storm runoff away from your basement, siding, and landscaping. We install seamless aluminum gutters, add leaf guards, and fix the ones that have started to sag or leak.

Why Gutters Matter So Much in Michigan Weather

Down here, gutters aren't a nice-to-have — they're the drainage system that keeps water off the most expensive parts of your house. Every spring and fall we get soaking rains, and every winter the roof sheds snowmelt that has to go somewhere. When gutters overflow or dump right against the house, that water finds the foundation, the basement, and the wood behind your fascia.

Freeze-thaw cycles make it worse. Water that sits in a clogged or poorly pitched gutter freezes, expands, and helps feed the ice dams that back up under your shingles. A properly sized, cleanly pitched gutter system moves that water out and away before it can do any damage.

  • Carry snowmelt and heavy rain away from your foundation and basement
  • Reduce the standing water that feeds ice dams and fascia rot
  • Protect siding, trim, and window frames from constant runoff
  • Keep flower beds, walkways, and landscaping from washing out
  • Route water to downspouts placed where it actually drains away

Seamless Aluminum Gutters, Cut to Fit Your Home

We form seamless aluminum gutters on-site, running each length as one continuous piece to match your roofline. Compared to the sectional gutters sold in boxes at the hardware store, seamless means far fewer joints — and joints are exactly where leaks, clogs, and drips tend to start.

Most metro Detroit homes do well with K-style aluminum in a 5-inch profile, while larger roofs or steep pitches that shed a lot of water often call for 6-inch gutters and bigger downspouts. We'll recommend the size that matches your roof's real runoff, set the right pitch toward the downspouts, and place those downspouts where the water actually has somewhere to go.

  • Seamless aluminum formed on-site — no store-bought sections
  • K-style profiles in 5-inch and 6-inch sizes
  • Baked-on color finishes to match your fascia and trim
  • Correct pitch so water always runs toward the downspouts
  • Downspouts and extensions placed to drain away from the foundation
  • Hidden hangers for a clean look and a solid hold in wind and snow

Gutter Guards & Leaf Protection

If you're tired of climbing a ladder every fall — or watching your gutters overflow because they're packed with maple seeds and pine needles — guards are worth a look. Leaf protection covers the top of the gutter so debris sheds off while water still flows in, which matters a lot heading into a Michigan winter, where a clogged gutter quickly turns into a frozen one.

Guards aren't magic — no system is truly maintenance-free — but the right guard dramatically cuts how often your gutters need attention and helps them keep up with heavy fall leaves and spring runoff.

  • Keep leaves, needles, seeds, and roof grit out of the channel
  • Cut down on cleanings and ladder trips through the year
  • Help prevent the clogs that back up and feed ice dams
  • Options for brand-new gutters or a retrofit onto sound existing ones

Gutter Repair, Cleaning & Replacement

Not every gutter problem means a full replacement. If your system is basically sound but starting to act up, we can often fix what's wrong for a fraction of the cost of new. When gutters are rusted through, badly undersized, or pitched the wrong way from the start, replacement is the honest answer — and we'll tell you plainly which camp your home falls into.

  • Re-secure gutters that are sagging or pulling away from the fascia
  • Reseal leaking seams, corners, and end caps
  • Correct poor pitch so water stops pooling and overflowing
  • Replace damaged sections and worn or broken hangers
  • Clear out clogs and debris so the system drains freely again
  • Full seamless replacement when repair no longer makes sense

Gutters and Your Roof Work as One System

Your roof and your gutters are two halves of the same job: getting water off the house and away from it. That's why gutters are the natural companion to a roof replacement. When we're already up there tearing off old shingles, it's the ideal time to swap out tired gutters, correct drainage problems, and set everything up to handle Michigan's weather together.

If you're planning a new roof, ask us to look at your gutters at the same time. Coordinating both means clean flashing-to-gutter transitions, no mismatched leftover sections, and one local team standing behind the whole system with our workmanship guarantee.

Gutters & Guards — FAQs

How much do seamless gutters cost in metro Detroit?

Every home is different, but most seamless gutter projects around metro Detroit land in a typical range of about $6 to $14 per linear foot installed, with 6-inch gutters and guards on the higher end. A full home often runs a few thousand dollars total. We'll measure your roofline and give you a free, exact written estimate — no guessing.

What's the difference between seamless and sectional gutters?

Sectional gutters come in short pieces joined together, and every seam is a spot where leaks and clogs start. Seamless gutters are formed from one continuous piece of aluminum, cut to fit your roofline on-site, so the only joints are at corners and downspouts. That means far fewer leaks and a cleaner look that holds up to Michigan winters.

Do I really need gutter guards in Michigan?

They're worth serious consideration, especially under mature maples, oaks, and pines. Guards keep leaves, needles, and debris out so water keeps flowing instead of backing up and freezing into ice dams. They don't make gutters fully maintenance-free, but they cut cleanings way down and help your system handle heavy fall and spring runoff.

Can you repair my gutters instead of replacing them?

Often, yes. If your gutters are sagging, pulling away from the fascia, or leaking at a few seams, re-securing hangers, resealing joints, or swapping a section can buy you years. If they're rusted through, badly pitched, or undersized for your roof, replacement is the smarter spend. We'll tell you honestly which one your home actually needs.

How long does a gutter installation take?

Most single-family gutter installations are a one-day job once your materials and color are set. Larger homes, steep rooflines, or projects that add guards can run into a second day. Because we form the gutters on-site, weather permitting we work efficiently — and when our schedule allows, we can often get you on the calendar quickly.
