Signs You Need a New Roof in Michigan

Michigan is hard on roofs. Freeze-thaw cycles work water under shingles, ice dams back up at the eaves, spring wind and hail storms tear shingles loose, and humid summers bake the protective granules off. Most asphalt roofs in our climate last about 20 to 25 years — and when they go, they rarely fail all at once.

Watching for the early warning signs is what separates a planned replacement from a middle-of-the-night emergency. If several of the items below sound familiar, it's worth having the roof looked at before the next storm.

  • Shingles that are curling, cupping, cracked, or missing after a windstorm
  • Bald spots and piles of shingle granules collecting in your gutters
  • Water stains on ceilings or walls, or daylight visible in the attic
  • A roof that's 20-25 years old, or one you know already has an overlay
  • Sagging areas or a spongy deck, which can signal trapped moisture and rot
  • Same-age roofs on neighboring homes all being replaced around you

Repair or Replace? Honest Guidance

Not every problem needs a full replacement. A few blown-off shingles or a single flashing leak on an otherwise healthy roof is usually a repair. But once the damage is widespread, the roof is past 20 years, or you're paying for the second repair in a single year, replacement is almost always the smarter money.

We'll tell you honestly which camp your roof is in — even when the answer is that you've got a few good years left. Our recommendation goes in writing so you can decide with clear information instead of pressure.

  • Repair makes sense: isolated damage, a younger roof, one clear problem area
  • Replace makes sense: age past 20-25 years, widespread curling, or repeat leaks
  • Repeated repairs on an aging roof usually cost more than one clean replacement
  • A prior overlay hiding the original roof is a strong reason to replace

What Goes Into a Proper Roof Replacement

A roof isn't just shingles — it's a layered system, and in Michigan every layer earns its keep. We tear off down to the wood deck so nothing rotten gets buried, then rebuild the roof the right way from the deck up. Long roof life comes from the parts you never see.

Ice-and-water shield at the eaves and valleys is the single most important defense against ice-dam leaks, and Michigan code calls for it in those vulnerable areas. Proper drip edge, step and chimney flashing, and balanced ridge-and-soffit ventilation keep water out and let your attic breathe so shingles don't cook from below.

  • Complete tear-off to the deck, with every board inspected and bad sheathing replaced
  • Ice-and-water shield at eaves and valleys to stop ice-dam and wind-driven leaks
  • Full synthetic underlayment — lighter, tougher, and more water-resistant than felt
  • New aluminum drip edge along eaves and rakes, plus step and chimney flashing
  • Balanced ridge and soffit ventilation to fight summer heat and attic moisture
  • A manufacturer-matched ridge cap to finish and seal the peak

Shingle Brands, Styles, and Colors

Most of our customers choose architectural — also called dimensional — shingles. They're thicker and heavier than old-style 3-tab shingles, hold up better against wind and hail, and give your roof depth and shadow lines that flat shingles simply can't. They also carry stronger manufacturer-backed material warranties.

We install shingles from the major manufacturers, so you're never locked into a single look. Whether you want a classic weathered wood, a deep charcoal, or a fresh color that lifts your curb appeal, we'll bring physical samples and help you match your home and your neighborhood.

  • A full range of colors, with real samples brought right to your home

Roof Replacement — FAQs

How much does a roof replacement cost in metro Detroit?

Every roof is different, but typical asphalt replacements in metro Detroit run roughly $6,000 to $16,000, depending on size, pitch, how many old layers we tear off, and the shingle line you choose. Larger or steeper homes can run higher. These are typical ranges only — the honest way to get your real number is a free on-site inspection and written estimate.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family asphalt roofs are torn off and fully re-installed in one day. Larger homes, steep pitches, or complicated rooflines can stretch into a second day. Weather matters too — we won't rush a roof on during rain or high wind. When you book a free inspection, we'll give you a realistic schedule for your specific home.

Should I tear off the old roof or just overlay new shingles?

We almost always recommend a full tear-off. An overlay — a second layer laid over the old shingles — is cheaper up front, but it traps heat and moisture, hides rotten decking, and adds weight your roof carries through heavy Michigan snow load. Tear-off lets us inspect the deck, install fresh underlayment and ice-and-water shield, and start clean.

Can I replace my roof in the winter?

Yes, roofs are installed year-round in Michigan. In cold weather we adjust our methods, hand-sealing shingles and handling brittle materials carefully so everything seals properly. If a leak is already spreading, waiting until spring often means more interior damage. When our schedule allows we can move quickly — book a free inspection and we'll advise you honestly.

Will insurance pay for my storm-damaged roof?

If a wind or hail storm damaged your roof, we can inspect it, document the damage, and work alongside your insurance adjuster through the claims process. To be clear: your deductible is your responsibility, and we never promise free work paid by insurance. We help you understand what's covered and give an honest assessment of the damage.

Architectural or 3-tab shingles — which should I choose?

Architectural (dimensional) shingles are our standard recommendation. They're thicker, more wind-resistant, longer-lasting, and carry stronger manufacturer warranties than flat 3-tab shingles — and they simply look better. We install shingles from major brands like GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed in a wide range of colors, and we'll bring samples to match your home during your free estimate.
