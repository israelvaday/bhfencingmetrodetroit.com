Roofing Livonia homeowners rely on

Livonia is a city of homeowners. Much of it was built out in a hurry between the 1950s and 1970s, and that history is still overhead: block after block of brick and aluminum-sided ranches, tri-levels and quad-levels spread across neighborhoods from Rosedale Gardens to Coventry Gardens and the subdivisions off Five, Six and Seven Mile. These are single-story and split homes with wide footprints, which means a lot of roof per house — and with owner-occupancy among the highest in Wayne County, most of those roofs belong to people who plan to stay put and want the job done once, done right.

The ranch layout that makes Livonia so livable also creates a specific roofing challenge. Long, low-slope roof runs shed snow slowly, and over a Michigan winter that sets the stage for ice dams — ridges of ice that form at the cold eaves, trap meltwater behind them and force it back up under the shingles and into the ceiling below. Plenty of Livonia roofs are also on their second or third layer of asphalt shingles, sitting over attics that were never vented or insulated to today's standards. When we inspect a leak here, the fix is often as much about airflow, insulation and gutters as it is about shingles.

Then there's the weather these roofs live through. Freeze-thaw swings crack aging shingles and work fasteners loose; heavy snow loads and lake-effect moisture test every flashing and valley; and spring brings the wind and hail that roll across southeast Michigan and strip shingles off in an afternoon. It all adds up to steady, year-round demand in Livonia for full roof replacements, targeted repairs, better attic ventilation and gutters that actually keep up — the everyday work we do best.

The roofing Livonia homes need most

For most Livonia homes, the roofing that matters most is a well-installed asphalt shingle replacement paired with the details that make it last: proper attic ventilation, ice-and-water shield along those vulnerable low-slope eaves, and gutters sized to move Michigan snowmelt away from the house. We handle full tear-offs and re-roofs, storm and leak repairs, flashing and valley work, ventilation upgrades and gutter replacement — and if your roof still has good years left, we'll tell you that instead of selling you a new one. Every roof we install carries manufacturer-backed material warranties plus our own workmanship guarantee.

Roofing in Livonia — FAQs

Do you serve Livonia?

Yes. Livonia is right in the heart of the area we cover. We're a metro Detroit service-area roofer working across Wayne, Oakland and Macomb counties, and Livonia — from Rosedale Gardens to the neighborhoods along Newburgh, Middlebelt and Farmington roads — is one of the communities we work in most. Request a free inspection and we'll come to you.

My roof is leaking after a storm — how fast can you get out here?

Active leaks jump to the front of our list. Send us a message through our free inspection request and we'll get a roofer to your home as soon as our schedule allows — often the same or next business day. We can talk you through simple steps to limit interior damage in the meantime, and when our crew arrives we'll find the source, protect the area and lay out your repair options. (Our regular hours are Sunday through Thursday 9am–5pm and Friday 9am–noon.)

How much does a roof replacement cost in Livonia?

Every roof is different, so treat this as a ballpark only: for a typical Livonia ranch or quad-level, most asphalt shingle replacements fall somewhere in the range of about $7,000 to $16,000, depending on the size and pitch of the roof, how many old layers have to come off, the shingle you choose, and any decking, ventilation or gutter work that comes with it. The only way to know your real number is a look at your actual roof — and that estimate is always free.

Why do Livonia roofs get so many ice dams — and can you fix it?

It's the low-slope ranch roofs combined with our winters. Warm air escaping into an under-ventilated attic melts snow on the upper roof; that water refreezes at the cold eaves and builds a dam that pushes moisture back under the shingles. We treat the cause, not just the symptom — adding attic ventilation and insulation, installing ice-and-water shield along the eaves during a re-roof, and making sure gutters and downspouts are clear and sized right. If ice dams keep staining your ceilings, book a free inspection and we'll show you what's happening up top.
