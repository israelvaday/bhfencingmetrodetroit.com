Roofing Clinton Township homeowners rely on

Clinton Township is Macomb County's most populous community, and its roofs are as varied as its neighborhoods — postwar brick ranches, mid-century colonials, and clusters of condominiums lining the Gratiot Avenue and Garfield Road corridors. A lot of this housing stock went up between the 1950s and the 1980s, which means plenty of roofs are now on their second or third layer of asphalt shingle and reaching the end of their service life. When shingles that age meet a hard Michigan winter, small problems turn into leaks fast.

Sitting just west of Lake St. Clair, Clinton Township catches the full Michigan weather mix. Freeze-thaw cycles work shingles loose all winter, ice dams back water up under the eaves and into the sheathing, heavy wet snow piles onto the low-slope ranch roofs that are so common here, and the spring wind-and-hail storms that sweep through Macomb County can strip granules and tear tabs in a single afternoon. Add humid summers and lake-effect moisture, and a roof around here earns every year you get out of it.

Because so many homes are packed into established subdivisions and condo associations, Clinton Township sees high volume in both quick repairs and full storm-damage claims. Whether you own a single-family ranch off Garfield, a colonial near the Clinton River, or you're a condo board coordinating a multi-unit re-roof, we scale the work to fit the job — and we keep you in the loop the whole way.

Roof replacement in Clinton Township, MI

If your roof is past patching, our Clinton Township roof replacement service handles everything in one project — we protect the property, tear off the old layers, install fresh underlayment, ice-and-water shield, and shingles, then sweep up every nail. From roof repair to roof replacement, replacement quotes in Clinton Township are always free and no-obligation.

The roofing Clinton Township homes need most

In Clinton Township the two jobs that come up most are storm-damage repair and full replacement of aging shingle roofs. That means fast leak tracing and shingle repair after spring wind and hail, ice-dam and flashing fixes through the freeze-thaw months, and complete tear-off-and-replace on roofs that are 25 years or older. New roofs go on with manufacturer-backed shingle systems, proper attic ventilation, and ice-and-water shield at the eaves — that last detail matters a lot on the low-slope ranch roofs that dominate the area, where ice dams do the most damage. We handle residential shingle roofs and commercial and flat roofs alike, and we're glad to walk condo associations through the bigger, multi-building projects.

Roofing in Clinton Township — FAQs

Do you serve Clinton Township?

Yes. Clinton Township sits right in the heart of our Macomb County service area, and we cover the whole community — from the Gratiot and Garfield corridors to the neighborhoods along the Clinton River and the condo developments in between. Request a free estimate and we'll come to you.

A storm just hit my roof — how fast can you come out?

When you've got an active leak or fresh storm damage, reach out and we'll get you on the schedule as quickly as we can — often same-day when our schedule allows. We'll document the damage for your insurance claim, get a temporary cover in place if the roof is open to the weather, and set up the permanent repair. Our hours are Sunday through Thursday 9am–5pm and Friday morning, so the sooner you message us, the sooner we can help.

How much does a roof replacement cost in Clinton Township?

Every roof is different, but for a typical Clinton Township single-family home, full asphalt shingle replacement usually lands somewhere around $8,000 to $18,000 — depending on the roof's size and pitch, how many old layers have to come off, and the shingle system you choose. Larger, steeper, or more complex roofs run higher. Those are typical market ranges, not a quote; the only way to get a real number is a free on-site estimate, which is exactly what we offer.

Can you help with a storm-damage insurance claim?

We can. We'll inspect the roof, photograph and document any hail or wind damage, and work alongside you and your adjuster through the claims process so nothing gets overlooked. To be straight with you: your deductible is still your responsibility. What we do is make sure the damage is fully and accurately represented so your claim reflects the real scope of the work.
