Roofing Dearborn homeowners rely on

Drive through Dearborn and you'll see block after block of solid, older homes — 1920s and '30s brick bungalows, mid-century colonials, and story-and-a-half houses in the neighborhoods around Ford's headquarters and out toward west Dearborn. They were built to last, and many still wear roofs that are well past their prime. Once an asphalt roof pushes past 20 years, the shingles turn brittle, granules wash down into the gutters, and the flashing around chimneys and dormers starts letting water sneak in.

The other thing Dearborn has in abundance is trees. That mature canopy is one of the nicest things about the city, but it's tough on roofs. Overhanging limbs drop leaves, twigs, and seed pods that pile up in the valleys and clog gutters, while constant shade keeps north-facing slopes damp long enough for moss and dark algae streaks to take hold. Left alone, that trapped moisture works its way under the shingles and shortens the life of the whole roof.

Then there's Michigan weather doing what it does. Winter brings freeze-thaw cycles and ice dams that back water up under the shingles along the eaves; heavy snow loads lean on older decking; and spring rolls in with the wind and hail that tear at aging shingles. Add humid summers and lake-effect moisture on top, and it's easy to see why so many Dearborn homeowners end up chasing a stubborn leak — or deciding it's finally time for a new roof.

Roof replacement in Dearborn, MI

Thinking about a roof replacement in Dearborn? We'll walk you through shingle choices, ventilation, and real pricing before you commit — and if a repair will honestly do the job, we'll say so. Most Dearborn re-roofs, including roof repair and roof replacement, are completed fast so your home isn't exposed to Michigan weather.

The roofing Dearborn homes need most

For most Dearborn homeowners, the work falls into a few buckets. First is honest leak repair — finding where the water is actually getting in (usually failed flashing, open valleys, or a handful of worn shingles) instead of just painting over the stain inside. Second is full re-roofing for the many homes whose original or second-layer roofs have simply reached the end of the road; a clean tear-off, fresh underlayment, and proper attic ventilation make a real difference on these older houses, especially when it comes to fighting ice dams. We also clear the moss and debris that all that tree cover leaves behind, and because Dearborn's aging homes often show their years on the walls as well as the roof, we handle siding too — so the whole exterior is buttoned up against the weather.

Roofing in Dearborn — FAQs

Do you serve Dearborn?

Yes — Dearborn sits right in the heart of our service area. We're a metro Detroit roofing team covering Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties, and Dearborn, from the east-side neighborhoods near Ford to the west-side colonials, is a community we work in regularly. Request a free estimate and we'll come take a look.

How fast can you come out after a storm or a leak?

When there's an active leak, we treat it as a priority and move quickly to stop the water and protect your home. Same-day visits are sometimes possible when our schedule allows, and otherwise we'll get you on the calendar as soon as we can. Our office hours are Sunday through Thursday 9am–5pm and Friday 9am–12pm. Reach out through our free estimate request and tell us what's happening — the more detail you give us, the faster we can help.

What does a roof replacement typically cost in Dearborn?

Every roof is different, but as a rough guide a full asphalt-shingle replacement on a typical Dearborn home usually lands somewhere in the $7,000–$15,000 range, depending on the size and pitch of the roof, the materials you choose, and whether old layers need to be torn off. Smaller leak repairs often run from a few hundred dollars up to around $1,500. Those are typical ranges only — the only way to know your real number is a free, no-obligation estimate.

My roof was damaged in a storm — can you help with my insurance claim?

We can. If wind, hail, or an ice storm damaged your roof, we'll inspect it, document the damage with photos, and walk you through what we find so you can decide whether to file a claim. If you do, we'll work alongside your insurance adjuster through the process. Just know that your deductible is your responsibility — we can't waive or cover it — but we'll make sure the scope of work reflects the real damage.
