Roofing Canton homeowners rely on

Canton grew up fast. A lot of the township's housing stock went in during the big western-Wayne-County build-out of the 1990s through the 2010s — subdivision after subdivision of colonials, ranches, and two-stories put up on a tight schedule with builder-grade asphalt shingles. Those roofs were never meant to last forever, and now whole streets are hitting the same 20-to-30-year wall at the same time. If your neighbors' dumpsters and re-roof crews seem to be multiplying, that's why: Canton is in the middle of a simultaneous re-roof and siding wave, and your roof is likely on the same clock.

Canton's flat, open terrain doesn't do those aging roofs any favors. There's little tree cover or hill to break the wind on many of these newer subdivisions, so spring wind and hail storms hit shingles head-on and lift the ones that are already brittle. Add Michigan's freeze-thaw cycle — water works into a hairline crack, freezes, expands, and pries it wider every night all winter — and a roof that looked fine in September can be leaking by March.

Winter is where the tired builder-grade roofs really show their age. Heavy snow load sits on low-slope sections, and ice dams form along the eaves when attic heat melts snow that refreezes at the cold edge — backing water up under the shingles and into the ceilings below. Humid Metro Detroit summers and lake-effect moisture keep the deck damp and speed up rot in any spot where the flashing or ventilation was cut short during the original build. It all adds up to the same story across Canton: roofs that were fine for two decades and are now ready for their first real replacement.

Roof replacement in Canton, MI

If your roof is past patching, our Canton roof replacement service handles everything in one project — we protect the property, tear off the old layers, install fresh underlayment, ice-and-water shield, and shingles, then sweep up every nail. From roof replacement to roof repair, replacement quotes in Canton are always free and no-obligation.

The roofing Canton homes need most

The service Canton needs most right now is a full tear-off and roof replacement — those original 1990s-2010s subdivision roofs are past patching, and a proper re-roof lets us fix the things the original crews rushed: adding a full ice-and-water shield along the eaves and valleys to stop ice-dam leaks, correcting attic ventilation so heat and moisture actually escape, and replacing any soft decking underneath. We still do plenty of targeted roof repair in Canton too — sealing storm-lifted shingles, re-flashing chimneys and skylights, and chasing down a stubborn leak — and we'll always tell you honestly when a repair will hold and when your dollars are better spent on a replacement backed by manufacturer material warranties and our workmanship guarantee.

Roofing in Canton — FAQs

Do you serve Canton?

Yes. Canton is right in our western-Wayne-County service area, and it's one of the busiest — a lot of the township's subdivision roofs from the 1990s-2010s build-out are aging out at the same time. We cover Canton and the surrounding metro Detroit suburbs across Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties. Request a free estimate and we'll come take a look.

A storm just hit and my roof is leaking — how fast can you come out?

Reach out as soon as you spot it and we'll get you on the schedule as quickly as we can, often same-day when our schedule allows. In the meantime, contain any interior water with a bucket and keep people away from sagging or stained ceilings. Our hours are Sunday through Thursday 9am-5pm and Friday 9am-12pm; send us a message anytime and we'll respond the next business day if it's after hours.

What does a roof replacement typically cost in Canton?

Every roof is different, but for a typical Canton subdivision home an asphalt-shingle tear-off and replacement usually lands somewhere in the $8,000-$18,000 range depending on size, roof pitch, the shingle line you choose, and what we find under the old shingles. These are typical ranges, not a quote — the only way to get a real number is a free on-site inspection, which we're happy to do.

I think I have storm or hail damage. Can you help with my insurance?

Yes. We'll inspect the roof, document the damage with photos, and walk the findings through the claims process with you and your adjuster so nothing gets missed. To be upfront: your deductible is your responsibility — we can't waive it or promise insurance will cover everything — but we'll give you an honest assessment so you can decide on your claim with the full picture.
