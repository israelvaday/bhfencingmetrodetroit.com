Roofing Ferndale homeowners rely on

Ferndale is one of metro Detroit's tightest, oldest inner-ring suburbs — block after block of 1920s and 1930s bungalows and brick colonials on compact lots just off Woodward. A huge share of these homes still carry roofs that were sized for a different era: shallow attics, limited ventilation, and original wood decking under two or three layers of old shingles. That combination is exactly what struggles most through a Michigan winter, when snow load sits on the roof for weeks and freeze-thaw cycles work every seam loose.

Because Ferndale is a renovation town — additions, dormers, second-story pop-tops, kitchen and bath remodels seem to be happening on every street — a big part of our work here is remodel re-roofs and tie-ins. When you open up a wall or raise a roofline, the old and new sections have to be flashed and integrated so they shed water as one roof, not two. We also see a lot of tear-offs where layered shingles have to come all the way down to the deck so we can inspect the sheathing, replace anything soft, and start clean.

The other Ferndale constant is ice dams. On these low-slope, under-ventilated older roofs, heat escaping the living space melts snow up top; it refreezes at the cold eaves and backs water under the shingles and into the ceilings below. Pairing a proper re-roof with ice-and-water shield at the eaves, better attic ventilation, and seamless gutters that actually carry meltwater away is the difference between a roof that fights winter and one that just leaks through it.

Roof replacement in Ferndale, MI

When Ferndale homeowners ask whether it's time to replace their roof, we look at the shingles, flashing, valleys, and ventilation — not just the surface. If replacement wins, you get an exact written quote covering materials, labor, and cleanup. We handle everything from roof repair to roof replacement across Oakland County.

The roofing Ferndale homes need most

Most Ferndale homes need one of a few things, and we do all of them: full roof replacement and tear-offs on aging bungalows where layered shingles have reached the end; targeted roof repair for storm-damaged, missing, or curling shingles and worn flashing around chimneys, dormers, and old plumbing stacks; and remodel re-roofs that cleanly tie a new addition or raised roofline into the existing roof. Given the neighborhood's ice-dam history, we put real emphasis on the details that protect older roofs through a Michigan winter — ice-and-water shield at the eaves, improved attic ventilation, and seamless gutters sized to move heavy snowmelt and spring rain off the house before it can pool. Everything we install is backed by manufacturer-backed material warranties and our own workmanship guarantee.

Roofing in Ferndale — FAQs

Do you serve Ferndale?

Yes. Ferndale is right in our core service area. We're a metro Detroit roofing company covering Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties within about 35 miles, and Ferndale's older Woodward-corridor housing stock is exactly the kind of work we do every week. Reach out and we'll set up a free on-site look.

How much does a roof replacement in Ferndale cost?

Every roof is different, so we only give firm numbers after we've been on yours. As a rough guide, a typical Ferndale bungalow or colonial re-roof often lands somewhere in the range of about $7,000 to $16,000 depending on size, pitch, how many old layers have to come off, and any deck repair we find. These are typical ranges, not a quote — request a free estimate for an exact price on your home.

I have an active roof leak after a storm — how fast can you come out?

As fast as our schedule allows, and we make storm and active-leak calls a priority. We're open Sunday through Thursday 9am to 5pm and Friday morning, and same-day inspections are sometimes possible when there's an opening. If water is coming in right now, get a bucket under it and message us with photos so we can advise on a temporary cover until we can get on the roof.

My roof was damaged by wind or hail — can you help with my insurance claim?

Yes. We'll inspect the roof, document the storm damage thoroughly, and work with you and your adjuster through the claims process so nothing gets missed. To be clear and honest: your deductible is your responsibility, and we never promise to cover or waive it. What we do is make sure the damage is properly represented so your claim reflects the real scope of work.
