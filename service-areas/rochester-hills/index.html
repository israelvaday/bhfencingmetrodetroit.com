Roofing Rochester Hills homeowners rely on

Rochester Hills is a city of large two-story colonials, brick-front traditionals, and custom homes tucked into wooded, rolling lots off roads like Adams, Tienken, and Walton. Many of these neighborhoods went up from the 1980s through the early 2000s, which means a lot of original roofs are now 20 to 30-plus years old and reaching the end of their service life all at once. When a roof this size fails, it's usually not the whole field of shingles that goes first — it's the valleys, the step flashing where a wall meets the roof, and the pipe boots that dry out and crack.

These homes tend to have complicated rooflines: multiple gables, dormers, steep pitches, and long valleys that funnel a lot of water and snow into a few concentrated spots. That's exactly where Michigan weather goes to work. Freeze-thaw cycles all winter pry at aging flashing, ice dams build along eaves and in those valleys and push meltwater back up under the shingles, and heavy snow load sits on the north-facing slopes that never see much sun.

The wooded lots that make Rochester Hills so appealing add their own wear. Mature trees drop limbs and debris that clog valleys and gutters, and the shade holds moisture, so you'll often see dark algae streaks and moss creeping across the shaded slopes long before the sunny side shows any age. Then come the spring wind and hail storms that roll across Oakland County and can lift, crack, or bruise shingles across a whole exposure in a single afternoon.

Roof replacement in Rochester Hills, MI

When Rochester Hills homeowners ask whether it's time to replace their roof, we look at the shingles, flashing, valleys, and ventilation — not just the surface. If replacement wins, you get an exact written quote covering materials, labor, and cleanup. We handle everything from roof replacement to metal roofing across Oakland County.

The roofing Rochester Hills homes need most

Because of the size and complexity of these homes, Rochester Hills leans heavily on full roof replacements and detailed repair work rather than quick patches. On a steep colonial, the details are everything — properly woven or metal-lined valleys, correct step and counter flashing at every wall and chimney, fresh pipe boots, and balanced intake-and-exhaust attic ventilation that helps fight the ice dams these rooflines are prone to. Many homeowners here want their new roof to match the architecture, so we install premium architectural (dimensional) shingles that read well on a big elevation, and standing-seam or accent metal roofing over porticos, bay windows, and steep front gables where owners want a custom look that also sheds Michigan snow beautifully. Everything is backed by manufacturer-backed material warranties and our own workmanship guarantee.

Roofing in Rochester Hills — FAQs

Do you serve Rochester Hills?

Yes. Rochester Hills sits right in the core of our Oakland County service area, and we cover the surrounding communities across Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties within about 35 miles of metro Detroit. Reach out through our site and we'll set up a free roof inspection and estimate at your home.

A storm just hit and my roof is leaking — how fast can you get here?

We move active leaks to the front of the line and will get out to you as soon as our schedule allows, sometimes the same day. Our office hours are Sunday through Thursday 9am-5pm and Friday 9am-12pm, and we're closed Saturdays, so if you reach us outside those hours we'll respond first thing the next business day. In the meantime, try to contain any interior dripping and keep people clear of sagging or stained ceilings.

How much does a roof replacement cost on a large Rochester Hills colonial?

Typical metro Detroit asphalt roof replacements often land somewhere in the low-to-mid five figures, and the bigger, steeper, more cut-up rooflines common in Rochester Hills — especially with premium architectural shingles or metal accents — can run higher. Every roof is different, though: pitch, layers to tear off, and flashing all affect the number, so the only honest figure is a free on-site estimate. We'll measure your roof and give you an exact, itemized price with no pressure.

Can you help with an insurance claim for storm or hail damage?

Yes. We can inspect the roof, document wind or hail damage with photos, and walk the findings through the claims process alongside you and your adjuster. To be clear and honest: your deductible is your responsibility — we never waive, cover, or discount it, and any offer to do so would be against Michigan law. What we do is make sure the damage is properly documented and the repair is done right.
