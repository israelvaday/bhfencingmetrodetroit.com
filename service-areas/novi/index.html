Roofing Novi homeowners rely on

Novi grew up fast. A large share of the housing stock went up during the building boom that followed Twelve Oaks and the I-96 corridor — big two-story colonials, brick-and-siding executive homes, and newer luxury builds packed into subdivisions off Beck, Wixom, and Ten Mile roads. Many of those roofs were laid with builder-grade three-tab shingles that are now well past the 18-to-22-year mark, right in the window where Michigan winters start to win. If dumpsters and permit signs are showing up down your street, there's a reason: whole neighborhoods in Novi were roofed around the same time, and they're all aging together.

Novi's rooflines also take a specific kind of beating. The steep, multi-plane roofs and tall front gables that give these homes their curb appeal create long valleys and low-slope transitions where snow piles up and ice dams form along the eaves during our freeze-thaw swings. Add lake-effect moisture drifting off Walled Lake and the Wixom lakes, humid summers baking the south-facing slopes, and the spring wind and hail that rip through western Oakland County, and you get roofs that need real flashing details and proper attic ventilation — not just a fresh layer of shingles slapped on top.

In the newer, warranty-minded parts of town, homeowners tend to want more than the bare minimum. We see a lot of demand around Novi for premium architectural shingles and standing-seam metal, paired with matching siding and gutter protection so the whole exterior holds up as one system. Older homes closer to downtown Novi and Walled Lake, meanwhile, often need tear-offs down to the deck, replaced valleys, and ventilation brought up to date. Either way, we walk the roof, show you what we find, and match the fix to the house.

Roof replacement in Novi, MI

Not every roof can — or should — be torn off. When years of Oakland County freeze-thaw, ice dams, and summer heat have worn out your shingles, we'll tell you straight and quote a full roof replacement in Novi at an honest, written price. You'll get clear repair-versus-replace advice, plus options across roof replacement and metal roofing that fit your home and budget.

The roofing Novi homes need most

Most Novi calls fall into one of two buckets. First is full roof replacement — many original subdivision roofs are simply timed out, and homeowners here often upgrade to dimensional architectural shingles or standing-seam metal for the longer life and the manufacturer-backed material warranties that come with a proper install. Second is targeted repair: wind-lifted and missing shingles after a storm, leaks tracing back to failed valley or chimney flashing, and ice-dam damage along the eaves each winter. Because Novi's homes lean toward steep, complex rooflines, the flashing, valley, and ventilation work is where roofs are won or lost, so we pay close attention there and back our work with a workmanship guarantee. We also handle siding and gutter protection when it makes sense to treat the exterior as one weather envelope rather than a patchwork.

Roofing in Novi — FAQs

Do you serve Novi?

Yes. Novi is right in our service area — we cover it along with the rest of western Oakland County and the metro Detroit suburbs across Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb. We're a service-area business, so we come to you: request a free estimate and we'll schedule a visit to walk your roof.

How fast can you get out after a storm or a leak?

As quickly as our schedule allows — after a big Michigan wind or hail event we do our best to prioritize active leaks and exposed roofs, and same-day visits are sometimes possible when there's an opening. We're not a 24/7 emergency line, but if water is coming in, send us a message with photos and we'll get you on the calendar fast and talk you through protecting the interior in the meantime.

Should I repair my roof or replace it?

It depends on the roof's age and how widespread the damage is. If your Novi home still has its original builder-grade shingles from the 1990s or 2000s, a replacement often makes more sense than chasing repeated leaks. If the roof is newer and the damage is localized — a wind-torn section, worn flashing — a repair is usually the right call. We'll give you an honest recommendation after we inspect it, not a one-size-fits-all upsell.

What does a new roof typically cost in Novi?

Every roof is different, but typical metro Detroit replacements often land somewhere in the low-to-mid five figures depending on size, pitch, the number of valleys, and whether you choose architectural shingles or metal. Novi's larger, steeper homes tend toward the higher end. These are typical ranges only — the only way to know your number is a free, exact on-site estimate, which we're happy to provide.
