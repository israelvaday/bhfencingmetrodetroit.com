Shingles scattered across the yard after the last blow off the lake? A drip that shows up every time it rains hard, or a ceiling stain that keeps creeping across the drywall? Living this close to Lake St. Clair, your roof works harder than most — and when it starts letting go, you want someone local who can pin down the leak and fix it right the first time. That's what we do.

Roofing St. Clair Shores homeowners rely on

St. Clair Shores is a mature, fully built-out lakeside suburb, and its roofs tell that story. The vast majority of homes went up during the postwar boom, roughly from the late 1940s through the 1960s — brick ranches, bungalows, and Cape Cods on neat lots between Nine Mile and Thirteen Mile. A lot of these houses are now on their second or third roof, and many of the asphalt shingle roofs installed back in the 1990s and 2000s are sitting at or past the end of their service life. When they start to fail, they tend to fail all at once.

Then there's the water. The city runs the full length of Lake St. Clair, and the Nautical Mile along Jefferson plus the canal neighborhoods reaching toward the lake put hundreds of homes directly in the path of open-water wind. Gusts rolling in off the lake catch roof edges, lift and crease shingles, and tear at ridge caps and flashing — which is exactly why wind- and storm-damage repair and full re-roofs are some of the most common calls we get in St. Clair Shores. It's the lakeside homes on and around the Nautical Mile that take the hardest hits.

Michigan's climate does the rest. Freeze-thaw swings work shingles and seams loose over the winter, ice dams build along the eaves and back water up under the shingles, heavy wet snow loads the deck, and the humidity coming off the lake keeps roofs and attics damp well into summer. On the older homes off Harper and Greater Mack — many with low-slope garage and porch additions and undersized attic ventilation — that combination ages even a decent roof faster than folks expect.

Roof replacement in St. Clair Shores, MI

When St. Clair Shores homeowners ask whether it's time to replace their roof, we look at the shingles, flashing, valleys, and ventilation — not just the surface. If replacement wins, you get an exact written quote covering materials, labor, and cleanup. We handle everything from roof repair to roof replacement across Macomb County.

The roofing St. Clair Shores homes need most

Because so much of St. Clair Shores sits in the wind path off Lake St. Clair, the work homeowners here need most is wind- and storm-damage repair — re-setting or replacing lifted and creased shingles, sealing failed flashing around chimneys and vents, and fixing torn ridge caps before water finds its way in. On the many postwar homes now on an aging roof, full asphalt shingle replacement is the bigger job we handle: a clean tear-off, a close look at the decking underneath, proper ice-and-water shield along the eaves to fight ice dams, and balanced attic ventilation so the next roof actually lasts. We also do targeted leak repair, gutter and soffit work, and low-slope repairs on the garage and porch additions that are so common in these neighborhoods. Not sure whether you need a repair or a full replacement? We'll tell you straight after a free inspection — no pressure either way.

Roofing in St. Clair Shores — FAQs

Do you serve St. Clair Shores?

Yes — St. Clair Shores is right in our service area. We're a metro Detroit roofing company covering Macomb, Oakland, and Wayne counties, and we work throughout the city, from the Nautical Mile and the canal neighborhoods to the streets off Harper, Greater Mack, and Jefferson. Reach out and we'll set you up with a free estimate.

My roof is leaking after a storm — how fast can you get out here?

Get in touch the moment you spot it and we'll get you on the schedule as fast as we can. When our schedule allows, we can often make it out the same day for active leaks and fresh storm damage. Our hours are Sunday through Thursday 9am-5pm and Friday 9am-12pm (closed Saturday) — message us through the site anytime and we'll respond during business hours to book your free inspection.

How much does a new roof cost in St. Clair Shores?

These are typical ranges — every roof is different. For a standard asphalt shingle replacement on an average St. Clair Shores home, most projects land somewhere around $8,000 to $18,000, depending on the roof's size and pitch, how many old layers we tear off, and the condition of the decking underneath. Larger or steeper roofs and premium materials run higher. The only way to know your real number is a free, no-obligation inspection and a written estimate.

Can you help with a wind or hail damage insurance claim?

Yes — storm claims are common around the lake. We'll inspect the roof, document any wind or hail damage with photos, and walk the findings through with you and your adjuster so nothing gets missed. Just know your policy and deductible are between you and your insurance carrier — we can't waive or cover a deductible, but we'll give you honest documentation and quality work so your claim goes toward a roof that holds up.
