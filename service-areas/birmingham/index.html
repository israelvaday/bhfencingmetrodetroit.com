Roofing Birmingham homeowners rely on

Birmingham's housing stock is a study in contrasts. Walk through neighborhoods like Quarton Lake, Poppleton Park, or Pembroke and you'll find storybook Tudor Revivals and Colonials from the 1920s through the 1940s — steep pitches, clustered dormers, and layered rooflines that were never meant for a quick, cookie-cutter reroof. A few streets over, a tired bungalow has been torn down and replaced with a large new custom home. Both extremes ask a lot of a roof, and both are exactly the kind of work we're set up to handle.

Many of Birmingham's original homes are now on their second or third roof, and the material matters as much as the labor. Those steep Tudor slopes were often built for slate or cedar shake; today most owners want that look without the weight or the price, so slate-look composite, dimensional architectural asphalt, and standing-seam metal are all popular here. On the newer custom builds, we regularly work around designer details — copper accents, wide valleys, low-slope porch tie-ins — that a production crew tends to rush. Getting the flashing, underlayment, and ventilation right on these complicated rooflines is where leaks are won or lost.

Then there's Michigan weather. Birmingham's mature tree canopy is beautiful, but overhanging limbs drop debris into valleys and trap moisture against shingles, and winter's freeze-thaw cycle pries at flashing and feeds ice dams along the eaves. Spring wind and hail move through Oakland County most years, and humid summers keep attics working overtime. On the older homes especially, tired flashing around chimneys and dormers is the first place we look when a ceiling stain shows up.

Roof replacement in Birmingham, MI

Thinking about a roof replacement in Birmingham? We'll walk you through shingle choices, ventilation, and real pricing before you commit — and if a repair will honestly do the job, we'll say so. Most Birmingham re-roofs, including roof replacement and metal roofing, are completed fast so your home isn't exposed to Michigan weather.

The roofing Birmingham homes need most

For most Birmingham homeowners, the work that matters most is high-end roof replacement done with an eye for the architecture — honoring the character of a historic Tudor or Colonial while upgrading to modern synthetic underlayment, ice-and-water shield along the eaves, and proper attic ventilation. Slate-look composite and standing-seam metal are in real demand here, alongside premium architectural asphalt on the custom builds. We also handle the smaller-but-urgent jobs: tracking down a stubborn leak, repairing storm and wind damage, replacing flashing around chimneys and skylights, and clearing the ice-dam trouble that comes with steep, shaded roofs. Whatever the scope, you get manufacturer-backed material warranties and our own workmanship guarantee.

Roofing in Birmingham — FAQs

Do you serve Birmingham?

Yes. Birmingham sits right in the heart of our service area in Oakland County, and it's one of the communities we work in most. We cover the city and its surrounding neighborhoods, from Quarton Lake to the downtown-adjacent streets. Reach out and we'll set up a free, no-obligation roof inspection.

How fast can you get out after a storm or a leak?

When a windstorm tears off shingles or a ceiling stain starts spreading, we move as quickly as our schedule allows — sometimes same-day, often within a day or two during busy stretches. We'll help you get a tarp or temporary protection in place to stop the water, then come back for a full assessment and a permanent repair. We're not a 24/7 operation, so the fastest way to reach us is to send an estimate request and we'll get you on the schedule right away.

What does a new roof cost in Birmingham?

Every roof is different, so these are typical ranges — not a quote. In the Birmingham market, a straightforward architectural-asphalt replacement often lands somewhere around $9,000 to $22,000 depending on size and complexity, while premium materials like standing-seam metal or slate-look composite — common on Birmingham's Tudors and custom homes — run meaningfully higher because of the material and the labor these steep, detailed rooflines demand. Multiple dormers, tall pitches, and designer details all factor in. The only way to know your real number is a free on-site estimate, and that's exactly what we provide.

My roof was damaged in a storm — can you help with insurance?

We can. If wind or hail damaged your roof, we'll document the damage thoroughly and walk the roof with your insurance adjuster so nothing gets missed. Keep in mind you're responsible for your deductible — anyone who tells you they'll cover or waive it isn't being straight with you, and in Michigan that isn't legal. What we will do is make sure your claim reflects the true scope of the damage and that the repair is done right.
