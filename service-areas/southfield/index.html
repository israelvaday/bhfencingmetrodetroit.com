Roofing Southfield homeowners rely on

Much of Southfield went up during the postwar boom, and it shows on the rooflines. Brick ranches, split-levels, and colonials from the 1950s through the 1970s fill neighborhoods off Evergreen, Southfield Road, and Twelve Mile — solid homes, but many now on their second or third roof. If yours still wears the asphalt shingles it came with, or a layer that was tacked on top decades ago, curling edges, bald granule-worn patches, and daylight in the attic are the roof telling you its time is up.

Southfield roofs take the full Michigan beating. Freeze-thaw cycles work at every nail and flashing joint all winter, ice dams build along shaded eaves and force meltwater back up under the shingles, and heavy snow load presses down on aging decking. Come spring, wind and hail storms strip shingles and bruise the mats, and humid summers plus lake-effect moisture leave the dark algae streaks and granule loss you see on so many older roofs around town. Small problems here don't stay small — a lifted shingle in November becomes a soaked ceiling by March.

Southfield is also unusual for how much flat roofing sits right alongside the houses. The Telegraph and Northland office corridor — the Golden Corridor stretch along Northwestern Highway — is packed with low-slope commercial buildings, and plenty of homes carry flat sections over additions, porches, and dormers. That means single-ply membrane systems like TPO and EPDM are just as much a part of roofing here as asphalt shingles, and a leak on a flat roof behaves nothing like one on a steep slope. We work on both.

Roof replacement in Southfield, MI

If your roof is past patching, our Southfield roof replacement service handles everything in one project — we protect the property, tear off the old layers, install fresh underlayment, ice-and-water shield, and shingles, then sweep up every nail. From roof repair to roof replacement, replacement quotes in Southfield are always free and no-obligation.

The roofing Southfield homes need most

For most Southfield homeowners, the bread and butter is full asphalt shingle roof replacement on those aging mid-century homes, plus targeted roof repair — flashing, valleys, pipe boots, and storm damage — to safely stretch more years out of a roof that isn't quite ready to go. But because the Golden Corridor and so many local homes carry flat and low-slope sections, TPO and EPDM membrane work is right up there too, alongside the ice-dam and attic-ventilation fixes Michigan roofs genuinely need. Whatever shape your roof is in, we'll match the right system to it and back the work with manufacturer-backed material warranties and our own workmanship guarantee.

Roofing in Southfield — FAQs

Do you serve Southfield?

Yes — Southfield sits right in our core service area. We cover the whole city, from the Northland and Telegraph corridor to the neighborhoods off Twelve Mile, Nine Mile, Evergreen, and Southfield Road, plus the rest of Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties within about 35 miles. Request a free estimate and we'll come out to you.

How fast can you respond after a storm or an active leak?

When a spring wind or hail storm rolls through, we know a torn-up roof can't wait. We prioritize active leaks and storm damage and can often get out for a free inspection the same day when our schedule allows. We'll tarp and stabilize the roof to stop water getting in, then walk you through the full fix. Our hours are Sunday–Thursday 9am–5pm and Friday 9am–noon.

What does a roof replacement cost in Southfield?

Every roof is different, but a typical asphalt shingle replacement on a Southfield home usually lands somewhere in the $8,000–$18,000 range depending on size, pitch, tear-off, and materials — that's a typical range, not a quote. Flat or low-slope TPO/EPDM work is priced differently. The only way to know your number is a free, no-pressure on-site estimate, and that's always exact and always free.

Do you handle flat and low-slope roofs, not just shingles?

Yes. Southfield's mix of homes and Golden Corridor commercial buildings means a lot of flat and low-slope roofs. We install and repair single-ply membranes like TPO and EPDM right alongside traditional asphalt shingle work, so whether it's a ranch, a colonial, or an office building, we've got the right system for it.
