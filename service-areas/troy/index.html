Roofing Troy homeowners rely on

Troy grew up fast after its 1955 incorporation, and most of the city's roofs sit on two-story colonials and brick ranches built through the boom decades of the 1970s, '80s, and '90s. A lot of those homes are now on their second or third roof, and the asphalt shingles laid down in the early 2000s are reaching the end of their service life — curling tabs, bald patches where the granules have washed away, and popped nail heads you can spot right from the driveway.

Closer to the Somerset Collection and Troy's premium subdivisions, the picture changes. These executive colonials carry steep, multi-facet rooflines — deep valleys, dormers, multiple gables, and long sightlines that show off every detail. Homeowners here tend to invest accordingly, choosing dimensional architectural shingles or standing-seam metal that stand up to the weather and match the character of the house. Complex roofs like these live and die by their flashing, valleys, and ventilation — that's where most leaks start, and where cut corners show up fast.

And southeast Michigan weather is relentless on a roof. Freeze-thaw cycles pry at every seam all winter, ice dams build along shaded eaves and force meltwater back up under the shingles, and heavy snow loads press down for weeks at a stretch. Then spring rolls in with wind and hail that can strip granules or crack older shingles outright. Add humid summers and lake-effect moisture, and a roof that isn't sealed and ventilated properly starts telling on itself — usually as a stain on a bedroom ceiling.

Roof replacement in Troy, MI

When Troy homeowners ask whether it's time to replace their roof, we look at the shingles, flashing, valleys, and ventilation — not just the surface. If replacement wins, you get an exact written quote covering materials, labor, and cleanup. We handle everything from roof replacement to metal roofing across Oakland County.

The roofing Troy homes need most

For most Troy homeowners, the work that matters most is full roof replacement on those aging 1980s-through-early-2000s asphalt roofs, plus storm-damage repair after the spring wind and hail season. On the larger executive homes, that often means stepping up to heavier architectural shingles or standing-seam metal, with careful attention to valleys, step flashing, and chimney and skylight details on complex multi-facet rooflines. Just as important is what goes on underneath: ice-and-water shield along the eaves and valleys, proper attic ventilation to cut down on ice dams, and clean, correct flashing work — the details that decide whether a roof lasts its full life through a Michigan winter. When a full replacement isn't what your roof needs yet, we also handle targeted repairs, missing-shingle fixes, and straightforward leak diagnosis.

Roofing in Troy — FAQs

Do you serve Troy?

Yes — Troy sits right in the heart of our service area. BH Roofing covers metro Detroit and the suburbs across Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties, and Troy, including the 48083, 48084, 48085, and 48098 zip codes, is well within range. Reach out and we'll set up a free on-site estimate.

A storm just hit my roof — how fast can you come out?

As soon as our schedule allows. After a big wind or hail storm we work through calls as quickly as we can, and same-day visits are sometimes possible when there's an opening. If you've got an active leak, set a bucket underneath it, move anything valuable out of the way, then send us photos and your address so we can get you on the schedule and help you protect the house in the meantime. Our office hours are Sunday through Thursday, 9am–5pm, and Friday, 9am–noon.

How much does a roof replacement cost in Troy?

Every roof is different, so the honest answer is that it depends on size, pitch, complexity, and the material you choose. As a rough guide, typical metro Detroit asphalt-shingle replacements tend to land somewhere in the $7,000–$16,000 range, while larger or more complex executive homes — and premium options like standing-seam metal — run higher. Those are typical ranges only, not a quote. The only way to get a real number for your roof is a free on-site estimate, which we're glad to provide.

Can you help with an insurance claim for storm damage?

We can. If wind or hail has damaged your roof, we'll inspect it, document the damage with photos, and walk the findings through with you so you understand exactly what you're looking at. We're happy to meet your insurance adjuster on-site and speak to the roofing specifics during the claims process. Keep in mind that your deductible is always your responsibility — we can't waive or cover it — but we'll help make the claim itself as straightforward as possible.
