Roofing Shelby Township homeowners rely on

Shelby Township did much of its growing during the building boom of the 1990s and 2000s, filling in large-lot subdivisions from Van Dyke and Schoenherr up toward Stony Creek Metropark. That means a big share of local homes are now wearing roofs that are 20 to 30 years old — right at the age when asphalt shingles start to curl, shed granules, and give up around the valleys and flashing. If your home went up in that era and still has its original roof, you are likely on borrowed time.

These aren't simple ranch roofs, either. Shelby's two-story colonials tend to have big footprints and busy rooflines — multiple gables, hips, dormers, and long valleys — and every one of those transitions is a place water wants to sneak in. Add Macomb County's weather and the wear adds up fast: freeze-thaw cycles that work shingles loose over the winter, ice dams building along eaves and in shaded valleys, heavy late-season snow load, and the spring wind and hail that roll through southeast Michigan and strip or bruise shingles in a single afternoon.

Up in the newer neighborhoods near Stony Creek, homeowners are increasingly skipping builder-grade three-tab and going premium — dimensional architectural shingles and standing-seam metal that stand up to wind, shed snow cleanly, and carry strong manufacturer-backed material warranties. Those larger, higher-pitched roofs deserve materials and installation to match, and that is exactly the kind of work we are set up to do.

Roof replacement in Shelby Township, MI

Not every roof can — or should — be torn off. When years of Macomb County freeze-thaw, ice dams, and summer heat have worn out your shingles, we'll tell you straight and quote a full roof replacement in Shelby Township at an honest, written price. You'll get clear repair-versus-replace advice, plus options across roof replacement and metal roofing that fit your home and budget.

The roofing Shelby Township homes need most

For most Shelby Township homeowners, the service in highest demand is full roof replacement — retiring those aging 1990s and 2000s asphalt roofs before a small leak turns into rotted decking and drywall damage inside. Right behind it is storm repair: wind-lifted and missing shingles, hail-bruised surfaces, and the ice-dam leaks that show up along eaves and in valleys after a hard Michigan winter. We also handle the fixes that quietly prevent all of the above — proper attic ventilation and insulation to keep ice dams from forming, valley and flashing repairs, and upgrades to architectural or standing-seam metal for owners who want to roof once and be done. Whatever the job, we back the materials with manufacturer-backed warranties and our labor with a workmanship guarantee.

Roofing in Shelby Township — FAQs

Do you serve Shelby Township?

Yes — Shelby Township sits right in the heart of our Macomb County service area. We work throughout all three local ZIP codes (48315, 48316 and 48317), including the neighborhoods near Stony Creek Metropark, and out to nearby Utica, Washington Township and Macomb Township. Request a free estimate and we'll come to you.

I have an active leak or storm damage — how fast can you get out here?

Reach out through our free inspection request and we'll prioritize you. Active leaks and fresh storm damage go to the front of the line, and we can often get a crew out the same day when our schedule allows. If water is coming in right now, we'll get the roof tarped and watertight first, then plan the permanent repair. Our regular hours are Sunday through Thursday 9am-5pm and Friday 9am-12pm.

How much does a new roof cost in Shelby Township?

Every roof is different, so these are typical ranges only — not a quote. A full asphalt architectural shingle replacement on a typical Shelby Township home usually lands somewhere around $9,000 to $20,000, depending on size, roof pitch, how many layers we tear off, and how complex the rooflines are. Larger colonials and standing-seam metal roofs run higher. The only way to know your real number is a free on-site estimate, and we'll put it in writing.

Can you help if my roof was damaged in a storm and I want to file an insurance claim?

Yes. We'll inspect the roof, document the storm or hail damage with photos, and walk your insurance adjuster through what we find so nothing gets missed. To be clear and honest: your deductible is your responsibility — we never waive it or promise 'free' insurance work, which isn't legal in Michigan — but we'll make the claim process as straightforward as we can and give you an honest read on whether filing even makes sense.
