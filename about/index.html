Who We Are

BH Roofing is a family-minded, locally run roofing company serving homeowners and businesses across metro Detroit. We're not a national franchise with a call center three states away. We're your neighbors, working on the same streets we drive every day, on roofs that take the same Michigan winters ours do.

That local footing shapes how we treat every job. When we shake on a scope of work, we mean it. When we quote a price, there are no surprise line items waiting at the end. We'd rather tell you a shingle patch will hold you another season than talk you into a full tear-off you don't need yet. Honest work, plainly explained, is the whole reason people call us back and send their neighbors our way.

Whether it's a single steep asphalt roof over a bungalow in Wayne County or a flat commercial membrane in Oakland or Macomb, the standard doesn't change: do it right, protect the property, and leave the place cleaner than we found it.

How We Work

It starts with a free, no-pressure inspection. We get up on the roof, look in the attic when it helps, and show you what we find, photos and all, so you can see the problem the way we do. Then you get a clear written estimate in language that actually makes sense, not a pile of jargon meant to rush you into signing.

On the job, the details are where trust is won or lost. We show up when we say we will. We tarp and protect landscaping, siding, and walkways before the first shingle comes off. We run a magnet over the yard and driveway for stray nails before we leave, and we haul away every scrap of old material. Your gutters, your garden, and your kids' bare feet in the summer all matter to us.

We keep you in the loop from the first handshake to the final cleanup, so you're never left guessing what's happening on your own roof. Ready when you are, and if you need us sooner, we'll fit you in when our schedule allows.

Built for Michigan Roofs

A roof in metro Detroit fights a war most of the country never sees. Freeze-thaw cycles pry at flashing and open up cracks all winter long. Heavy snow load and ice dams back water up under shingles and into ceilings. Spring rolls in with wind and hail that strip granules and lift tabs, and summer piles on heat and humidity that punish underlayment and ventilation.

We build for that reality instead of pretending it away. That means real attention to ice-and-water shield in the valleys and along the eaves where ice dams form, proper attic ventilation to fight the heat and moisture that shorten a roof's life, and flashing done tight around every chimney, vent, and wall so lake-effect moisture has nowhere to sneak in.

Missing shingles after that last windstorm? A ceiling stain that keeps spreading? We'll find the leak and fix it right, because we know exactly what Michigan weather does to a roof and how to stand it back up against the next round.

We Stand Behind Our Work

A roof is only as good as the crew that installs it, so we back our work two ways. Your materials carry manufacturer-backed warranties, and our labor is covered by our own workmanship guarantee. If something isn't right with what we did, we come back and make it right.

We're a licensed and insured local team, which protects your home and your wallet if anything goes sideways during the project. It's the baseline any homeowner should expect, and we're glad to confirm the details before you ever commit to a thing.

No pressure, no gimmicks, just a straight assessment of your roof and an honest plan to protect it. Request your free estimate and let's take a look together.
