Financing Available

A roof doesn't wait for a good time to fail, and metro Detroit is especially hard on them. Freeze-thaw cycles crack aging shingles, ice dams push water back under the deck, heavy snow load stresses the whole structure, and spring wind and hail can peel a slope in a single afternoon. Every week a problem sits, moisture works deeper into the sheathing, insulation, and drywall — turning a manageable repair into a much bigger bill. Financing exists so the timing of your repair or replacement is set by your roof's condition, not by whatever happens to be in your checking account that month.

Here's how it works. We work with established third-party lenders who specialize in home-improvement financing. It starts with a free roof inspection and a clear, written estimate — no guesswork about the number. From there, you can apply to finance the work and pay it back in fixed monthly installments. Approval, your rate, and the terms available are set by the lender based on your credit and are offered on approved credit, so we can't promise a specific figure on this page. What we can promise is an honest price up front and a straight answer about your options.

Financing gets you the same quality work you'd get paying any other way: proper tear-off and installation, manufacturer-backed material warranties, and our own workmanship guarantee, handled by a licensed and insured local team. If your roof was hit by a storm and you're filing an insurance claim, a payment plan can also help you manage your deductible or any out-of-pocket gap while the claim is processed — your deductible is always your responsibility, and we never waive or cover it. Ready to see the real numbers? Request your free estimate and we'll walk you through the full cost and every way you can pay for it.

Flexible ways to pay

  • Monthly payment plans through trusted third-party lenders — move forward now and pay over time, on approved credit.
  • Low- and no-money-down options may be available on approved credit, so a sudden leak doesn't have to wait on your savings.
  • A range of term lengths to help you land on a monthly payment that fits your household budget.
  • Finance the whole job or just part of it — a full tear-off and replacement, or a targeted repair after a Michigan wind or hail storm.
  • Use a payment plan to help cover your insurance deductible or an out-of-pocket gap on a storm-damage claim (the deductible remains yours to pay).
  • Prefer to pay directly? We accept standard payment methods too — just ask when you book your free roof inspection.

How financing a roofing project works

  1. Get your free estimate. We inspect the roof, walk you through options at every price point, and give you a clear written quote — no obligation.
  2. Choose how to pay. Pay upfront, or ask about monthly payment plans through our third-party lending partners on approved credit.
  3. Quick application. The lender's application takes minutes, and most credit decisions come back fast.
  4. We get to work. Once approved, we schedule your roofing project — often within days.

Why metro Detroit homeowners finance their roofs

A new roof is one of the highest-return upgrades a Michigan home can get — better protection against ice dams and leaks, lower energy bills through the freeze-thaw months, and instant curb appeal. But roofs fail on their own schedule, not your budget's. Financing lets you fix a leaking, storm-damaged, or worn-out roof now, before a Michigan winter makes it worse, and pay over time instead of putting the project off another season.

It also means you don't have to settle. Homeowners who planned on the most basic shingle often find that for a modest monthly difference they can get the architectural shingles, upgraded ventilation, or metal roof they actually wanted. Roofing financing — FAQs

Can I finance a roof replacement in metro Detroit?

Yes. BH Roofing offers financing options through third-party lenders on approved credit, so you can replace or repair your roof now and spread the cost over comfortable monthly payments. Ask your estimator for current plans when you book your free estimate.

Does applying for roofing financing change the price of the job?

No. Your written quote is the same whether you pay upfront or finance. We quote the roof, materials, and labor first — then you choose the payment option that works for you. There's never a penalty for paying the job off early with our typical lender programs.

What roofing projects can be financed?

Most projects qualify — full roof replacement, larger repairs, metal and flat roofing, gutters, and siding. Bigger jobs like a complete tear-off and re-roof are where monthly payments help most, but many mid-size repairs can qualify too.

How do I get started with financing?

Book your free estimate first. Once you have your written quote, we'll walk you through the current financing options and the lender's short application — most decisions come back quickly, and approved projects can usually be scheduled right away.
